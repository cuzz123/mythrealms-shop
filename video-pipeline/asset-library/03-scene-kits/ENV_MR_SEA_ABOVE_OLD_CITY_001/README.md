## Continuity locks

- One narrow photorealistic British coastal old-city street across all three views.
- Wet limestone paving, weathered red-brown brick, restrained unbranded shopfront glass, and no readable brands or landmarks.
- The ocean is distant and suspended overhead, with its underside facing the city; natural daylight filters through the water.
- Storm-cyan/deep-ocean palette, cold neutral reflections, and one consistent diffuse light direction.
- Reverse-rain water travels from pavement toward the overhead sea. No downward rainfall is used as a visual rule.
- The repaired upward view uses an irregular continuous water stream rather than a bead or pearl chain.

## Rejection gates

Reject any take or derivative that introduces downward rain, a ground-level ocean, a contradictory street or sea height, multiple suns, fantasy castles, recognizable IP, readable signage, text, logos, a full Mother, magic beams, particle clouds, or a warm orange-teal grade.
