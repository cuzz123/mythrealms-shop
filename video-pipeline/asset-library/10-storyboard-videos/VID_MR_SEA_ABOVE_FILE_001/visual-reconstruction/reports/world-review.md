## Per-asset findings

### Scene 01 — City Beneath the Sea

Pass. The full-resolution ultra-wide master has the ocean underside spanning the city horizon and more than five independent scale cues: dense districts and civic landmarks, harbour/river and rail/road corridors, cranes and vessels, distant cliffs, rooftop foreground, tiny people, traffic/light points and layered depth. The replacement uses smaller irregular reverse-rain filaments grounded in roads, roofs, gutters, river and harbour, with broken upward paths rather than conventional waterspouts or tornado funnels. The portrait crop proof retains ocean, city, harbour, rooftop foreground and multiple scale cues in a useful 9:16 corridor. The Mother cue is an incomplete broad diffuse pressure band crossing multiple districts, separable from the wave texture at full resolution and containing no eye, face, limb, tentacle, teeth, head, torso or complete silhouette.

### Scene 02 — Evacuation Square

Pass. The crowd remains varied and readable without clone patterns, face duplication or smear. The upper environment is an unmistakable dark overhead ocean rather than ordinary storm sky. Multiple adults visibly hold removed necklaces, pearls or other jewellery away from bare necks/bodies; the gestures do not reduce to covering ears or faces. Irregular tapered suction originates at fountains, paving, gutters, umbrellas and coats and rises toward the ocean. Civic steps, buses, statues, towers and layered foreground/midground/background people provide the required scale and live-action context.

### Scene 03 — S01 Street Hook

Pass. The anonymous blonde stand-in is off-axis on the left and remains observational rather than a centred fashion pose. The right-foreground civilian has a bare neck and collarbones, with a complete closed necklace loop and pendant visibly held in one hand approximately 20–30 cm away from the neck/chest; no chain touches, crosses or encircles the neck. Ground-origin puddle/gutter suction and upward direction remain readable in the still, with the overhead ocean and street/crowd depth preserved. This frame makes no accepted-character or casting implication: the blonde is explicitly temporary, anonymous and not Candidate A, B or C.

### Scene 04 — Rooftops Under the Mother

Pass. The 14–18 mm rooftop view preserves layered slate/brick roofs, chimneys, towers, cranes, antennae and tiny people across multiple rooflines. The Mother is only a broad diffuse city-block-scale shadow/pressure distortion embedded in the ocean. No lobe resolves as a limb, eye, face, head, torso, tentacle, teeth, anatomy or complete silhouette. The lighting, depth and practical city detail remain coherent with the other world scenes.

### Scene 05 — Cliffs, Harbour, and Vertical Ocean

Pass after the round-3 anti-fountain review. The full-resolution final preserves the asymmetric wet cliff with tiny people and road vehicles, working harbour/river, dense illuminated British city and civic tower, cranes, ships, practical lights and the full overhead ocean in one ultra-wide frame. The water behavior is carried by non-repeating ragged near-vertical sheets/curtains and broken fragments. Multiple visible lower origins are dark concave harbour-surface troughs/depressions with stretched ripples, and the sheets continue upward into or toward the ocean underside. The still therefore reads as impossible ground-to-sky suction rather than ordinary rain or downward sediment. The prior smooth repeated fountain arcs, bright bases and water arches are absent; there are no conventional waterspout/tornado funnels, magical beams or glossy synthetic columns.

### Portrait crop and overview

Pass. The Scene 01 portrait proof is a useful 9:16 crop. The deterministic overview is a 2560×1440 black RGB canvas with labels `01`–`05` confined to black top gutters; all five scene previews are identifiable and the masters remain unchanged. The overview is a navigation/proof sheet, not a replacement for full-resolution review.
