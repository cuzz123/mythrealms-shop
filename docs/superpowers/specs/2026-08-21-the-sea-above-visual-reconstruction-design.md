# FILE 001 — Visual Reconstruction Design

Date: 2026-08-21  
Status: Approved design; implementation remains gated on user review of this written specification  
Project: Maverenne — `FILE 001: THE PEARL THAT ANSWERED`

## 1. Decision and Scope

The previously generated protagonist, old-city environment, first-frame set, and Hollywood style test are visually rejected. They read as AI concept art or oil-painted composites, the protagonist lacks star-level attraction, and the world does not communicate city-scale danger.

This reconstruction replaces the visual foundation before any director card or video generation continues. It produces:

- three new protagonist casting candidates;
- five new world-scale scene candidates;
- one casting overview and one scene overview;
- provenance, prompts, hashes, and independent visual QA.

No Seedance video, paid credit use, final director card, or publication is in scope. Existing rejected files remain audit history and must not be used as positive visual references.

## 2. Chosen Direction

Direction A is binding: grounded science-fiction disaster epic with prestige television performance and live-action photographic realism.

The images must feel captured on a real British production location with integrated practical and invisible VFX. They must not resemble oil painting, game concept art, fashion illustration, fantasy matte painting, synthetic poster art, or an AI beauty campaign.

### 2.1 Photographic language

- Real-location production stills with physically coherent depth, weather, scale, and exposure.
- ARRI Alexa 35 / 35 mm film character without copying a named film or television property.
- 14–18 mm rectilinear lens for world-scale panoramas; 28–40 mm spherical lenses for character and street coverage.
- Natural overcast skylight, practical shop and street lighting, soft highlight roll-off, subtle organic grain, restrained contrast, and believable atmospheric perspective.
- Neutral wet-stone, brick, skin, and cloth colours. Storm cyan appears only as motivated light transmitted through the overhead sea.
- Imperfect, asymmetrical blocking and layered foreground/midground/background action. No centred runway pose, perfect bilateral street symmetry, or pristine CGI arrangement.

### 2.2 Mandatory anti-AI constraints

Reject any candidate with:

- painterly or brushed texture, wax skin, airbrushed pores, plastic hair, over-sharpened edges, or uniform microtexture;
- excessive teal/cyan grading, glowing outlines, magic particles, glossy water sculptures, or concept-art fog;
- repeated faces, cloned extras, distorted hands, impossible architecture, duplicate chimneys/windows, or floating objects without narrative cause;
- poster posing, empty streets used to hide scale, over-symmetrical composition, or perfect central vanishing points;
- readable generated signage, logos, prices, watermarks, or direct imitation of named characters, actors, studios, films, or television series.

## 3. Protagonist Casting Reconstruction

Create three genuinely different fictional adult candidates. None may derive from the rejected protagonist face.

All candidates share these locks:

- unequivocally adult, 21–24 years old;
- striking star-level facial presence with a memorable silhouette and expressive blue or blue-green eyes;
- natural blonde hair with believable wet clumping and flyaways;
- tall-looking, fit, feminine hourglass/athletic proportions without anatomical exaggeration;
- realistic skin pores, fine lines, asymmetry, under-eye structure, and restrained rain-safe makeup;
- refined fitted camisole and structured high-waisted short skirt, premium materials, tasteful bare arms and legs, and practical understated footwear;
- calm intelligence, contained fear, and the ability to hold attention without exaggerated acting;
- no celebrity likeness, generic influencer face, lingerie styling, nightclub styling, or fantasy-warrior styling.

The three candidates should differ in facial architecture and screen energy:

1. **Candidate A — Cold intelligence:** ash blonde, sculpted cheekbones, direct gaze, restrained authority.
2. **Candidate B — Dangerous curiosity:** pale gold blonde, sharper eyes, more volatile emotional presence.
3. **Candidate C — Luminous resilience:** honey-ash blonde, softer facial planes, strongest emotional accessibility.

Each candidate is delivered as a live-action casting board containing:

- clean close portrait;
- right profile suitable for the Baroque Orbit earring;
- three-quarter medium/full-body view showing outfit and proportions;
- neutral observation and upward-recognition expressions;
- no jewellery in the base casting images.

The user selects exactly one protagonist before storyboard first frames resume.

## 4. World Reconstruction

The world is not a narrow street with a decorative water ceiling. It is a populated British coastal city physically trapped beneath an ocean whose underside spans the entire horizon.

Scale must be proved through multiple simultaneous references: districts, civic buildings, towers, vehicles, people, harbour infrastructure, birds or aircraft silhouettes, cloud depth, water caustics, and a Mother shadow large enough to cross several city blocks.

The overhead sea must have atmospheric distance, varying depth, suspended sediment, broad underwater light fields, and a readable boundary across kilometres. It must not look like glass, a swimming-pool roof, or a flat texture pasted onto the sky.

Reverse rain is distributed across the city. Water pulls from gutters, rooftops, roads, rivers, and harbour surfaces in irregular ground-to-sky filaments. No downward splash crowns, bead chains, perfect columns, or ordinary rain fields are accepted.

## 5. Five Scene Candidates

### Scene 01 — City Beneath the Sea

- 14–18 mm ultra-wide establishing panorama from an elevated civic rooftop.
- Entire coastal city, harbour, cathedral-scale landmarks, dense districts, and distant cliffs visible beneath the overhead ocean.
- Multiple reverse-rain filaments rise across kilometres.
- A vast incomplete Mother shadow crosses several districts; no body anatomy.
- Primary purpose: prove the unique world in one image.
- Deliver a widescreen master plus a documented 9:16 crop-safe composition test.

### Scene 02 — Evacuation Square

- Monumental public square with hundreds of readable but non-repeating civilians.
- People remove pearls and jewellery while water pulls upward from paving, fountains, gutters, coats, and umbrellas.
- Civic architecture and transport provide human scale.
- Primary purpose: disaster scale and social rule recognition.

### Scene 03 — S01 Street Hook

- 9:16 production frame using a 28–35 mm observational lens.
- Selected protagonist is off-axis and still while layered crowds flee.
- At least one civilian visibly holds removed jewellery away from the body.
- Several irregular pavement-to-sea water filaments make upward direction undeniable.
- Primary purpose: eventual first-frame replacement after casting selection.

### Scene 04 — Rooftops Under the Mother

- Low 14–18 mm rooftop view across chimneys, towers, cranes, and antennae.
- The Mother is represented only by an impossible moving shadow and pressure distortion in the sea underside.
- No eye, face, limb, teeth, tentacle, or complete silhouette.
- Primary purpose: scale, dread, and restraint.

### Scene 05 — Cliffs, Harbour, and Vertical Ocean

- Epic coastal panorama showing cliffs, harbour, city skyline, and overhead sea in the same frame.
- Rising river and harbour water reveal the vertical relationship between ground, city, and ocean.
- Tiny vessels and people supply scale without becoming focal subjects.
- Primary purpose: demonstrate that the phenomenon covers a world, not one street.

## 6. Generation and Review Workflow

1. Two independent Luna Max production tracks may work in parallel because their outputs are isolated:
   - character casting candidates;
   - environment scene candidates.
2. Built-in image generation is used one call per distinct image or targeted repair. No CLI/API fallback and no video generation.
3. Rejected protagonist and environment images may be inspected only as negative examples. Positive references are limited to the immutable Baroque Orbit product truth and the written world rules.
4. Every generated asset records its exact prompt, reference roles, source path, output path, dimensions, colour mode, hash, and repair history.
5. Main-agent visual inspection is followed by an independent Luna Max review for anatomy, realism, scale, continuity, and anti-AI failures.
6. The user receives:
   - all three full-resolution casting boards and a three-candidate overview;
   - all five full-resolution scenes and a five-scene overview.
7. No previous first frame becomes accepted merely because it is technically valid. The user explicitly selects one casting candidate and approves the world direction.

## 7. Acceptance Gates

### Character gate

- The user finds the selected candidate sufficiently attractive and distinctive.
- Face, age, body, hair, and wardrobe read as photographed rather than rendered or painted.
- The identity can remain stable in profile, full-body, fear, and upward-recognition views.
- The right ear can carry the exact Baroque Orbit geometry without redesign.

### World gate

- At least one scene is unmistakably ultra-wide and city-scale.
- The city, overhead sea, reverse rain, and Mother shadow coexist with coherent perspective and light.
- Multiple scale references make the phenomenon larger than one street or building.
- No oil-painting texture, synthetic poster composition, or obvious generative artefact survives.

### Continuation gate

Only after both user selections are explicit may production:

1. replace S01–S09 first frames using the selected casting and world masters;
2. present the rebuilt nine-frame set for user approval;
3. complete the director card after that approval;
4. request a separate action-time confirmation before any paid Seedance generation.

## 8. Preservation and Rollback

- Rejected assets are never silently overwritten or promoted. New candidates use a separate `visual-reconstruction` namespace until selected.
- Existing product-truth source images and hashes remain immutable.
- No paid credits are consumed during reconstruction.
- If the reconstruction fails its gate, the candidate namespace can be discarded without altering the last committed storyboard package.
