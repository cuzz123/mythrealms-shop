**Status:** Internal evidence pack; read-only research completed 2026-07-26. This document does not authorize publication, deployment, metadata/schema changes, external contact, product claims, or a customer-visible brand change.

**Reviewed inputs:** `maverenne-seo-draft-pack-01.md`, `maverenne-seo-draft-pack-02.md`, `maverenne-seo-draft-pack-03.md`, and `content-calendar.md` (the repository file corresponding to the requested content calendar). The six pages in scope are `/pearls/how-to-wear`, `/gifts`, `/pearls/care`, `/pearls/freshwater-pearls`, `/pearls`, and `/about`.

## Evidence rules

- External evidence is limited to GIA educational material, FTC consumer/business guidance, and official Google Search Central documentation. No marketplace listing, retailer blog, social post, AI summary, supplier page, or secondary SEO source is used.
- A general pearl source can support only general pearl education. It cannot establish that any store SKU contains a pearl, contains a natural or cultured pearl, is freshwater, has a particular treatment or origin, or has any stated quality.
- Product facts require the exact SKU's approved first-party specification record. Policy and checkout facts require the current approved first-party policy/checkout record for the relevant order. Neither is supplied or validated by this pack.
- Editorial styling and gifting suggestions are editorial judgment, not externally verified facts. They may be retained only when written as optional ideas and not as fit, comfort, suitability, popularity, health, value, or outcome claims.
- `可（限通用教育）` means the cited proposition may be retained during pre-publication review, subject to rechecking the source and preserving the stated boundary. It does **not** mean the page is approved to publish.
